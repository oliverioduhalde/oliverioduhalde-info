*Propagación* translates acoustic phenomena into matter. Sound frequencies are applied to copper plates using cymatic techniques, producing standing wave patterns in sand; those patterns are then cast as permanent bas-reliefs. The invisible geometry of sound becomes a physical, tactile object.
