*Omphalos* places a fern (*Nephrolepis exaltata*) at the center of a bio-electronic installation. EMG and capacitive sensors read the plant's bioelectric signals and translate them in real time into an 8-channel ambisonic sound field and a responsive DMX lighting environment. The fern becomes both instrument and composer.

Continuation of the *Biosonora* line (2007). Production supported by Mecenazgo GCBA.
