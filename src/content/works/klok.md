*Klok* reimagines the clock as a sonic sculpture. Twenty-four aluminum tubular bells — one for each hour — are struck by Arduino-controlled mechanisms at precise intervals. Time becomes audible: not as a mechanical tick but as a progression of resonances that fill and decay in space.

Production supported by Mecenazgo GCBA (2018).
