*Volvox* projects live microscopic footage of the *Volvox* microorganism — a colonial green algae that moves as a collective — while custom software translates its movement patterns into real-time sound. The work makes audible the choreography of life at a scale invisible to the naked eye.

Winner of the ArCiTeC Award (2014).
