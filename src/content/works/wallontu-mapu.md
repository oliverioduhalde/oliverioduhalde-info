*Wallontu Mapu* ("the four directions of the earth" in Mapudungun) is an experimental opera drawing on the cosmogonic narratives of the Mapuche people. A vocal quartet and chamber ensemble are interwoven with quadraphonic electronics to construct a sonic cosmology — where cardinal directions become timbres and ancestral time becomes form.

Supported by Beca Fundación Antorchas (2003–2005).
