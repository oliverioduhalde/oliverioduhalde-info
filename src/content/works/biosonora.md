*Biosonora* (2007) is the founding prototype of Duhalde's bio-installation research. A fern is wired with sensors that capture its bioelectric activity and translate it into sound — establishing the conceptual and technical framework later developed in *Omphalos* (2019) and *Volvox* (2014–2017).
