*MicRA Performance* is the performative dimension of the MicRA (Microscopía, Robótica y Arte) laboratory. In live sessions, microscopic footage of biological and material samples is projected and processed in real time alongside electronic sound, collapsing the boundary between scientific observation and artistic performance.

The laboratory MicRA → `/research/micra-lab`. The workshop Micropraxiscopio → `/research/workshops/micropraxiscopio`.
